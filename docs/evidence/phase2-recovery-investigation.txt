THE BUG WAS REAL AND IS FIXED
  A mission tool step took `sideEffecting` from the TEMPLATE. Nothing derived it from the
  tool's declared risk, so a WRITE/EXTERNAL/DESTRUCTIVE step could report sideEffecting:false
  — and the acceptance boundary records only side-effecting work. Same principle the risk
  gate already enforced (a template cannot lower a tool's declared risk); it could still
  lower sideEffectiveness, and that half was missing.
  FIX (core/src/runtime.ts, at mission creation — the authoritative point):
      impliedByRisk = tool.risk !== "READ"
      step.sideEffecting = impliedByRisk || step.sideEffecting   // may raise, never lower
  VERIFIED LIVE: the same probe step now reports sideEffecting = TRUE (it reported false
  before the fix), and the mission completed.
